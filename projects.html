Projects & community

Personal finance

2025–present

RavaFinance

Co-founder & Chief Technology Officer · Cameroon

I lead product engineering on a four-person founding team building an AI-powered personal finance platform for African consumers. RavaFinance brings bank, Mobile Money, and community-savings data into one view to help people understand and manage their finances.

We launched an Android beta in Cameroon with more than 120 users testing the platform.

Co-founder · Cameroon
